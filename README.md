# Tinglet

App Name: Tinglet

Base Price: Free to download. Purchase inside app- premium catalog.

Concept

A premium, elegant and exclusive hand-bell app.

Clean, modern and luxurious design with high-quality bells and realistic ringing sounds.

Main Screen (Home)

•  Large, beautiful central bell displayed in the middle of the screen.

•  The user rings the bell by shaking the phone (primary interaction) or by tapping the bell.

•  When the bell rings:

	•  Very loud, rich and realistic sound (like a real metal bell).

	•  The bell animates slightly (swings).

	•  Light haptic feedback.

•  The currently selected background is shown behind the bell.

Bell Collection

Accessible via a “Bells” tab.

Users can freely choose their favourite bell. The selected bell is saved and shown on the home screen.

Included for free with the base purchase:

•  A selection of classic elegant bells (gold, rose gold, silver, white, black, etc.)

In-app purchases (premium packs):

•  Christmas / Jul pack (bells with red ribbons, holly, berries, festive decorations)

•  Spring / Summer pack (bells with flowers, roses, blossoms and soft ribbons)

•  Autumn / Fall pack (bells with autumn leaves and warm tones)

•  Halloween pack

•  Other premium / exclusive bells

All bells should have clearly different shapes and designs.

High-quality, photorealistic 3D renders with beautiful lighting and materials.

Backgrounds

Accessible via a “Backgrounds” tab.

Large selection of beautiful high-resolution backgrounds the user can choose freely:

•  Soft gradients

•  Elegant marble and stone textures

•  Nature (flowers, forest, snow, autumn leaves, soft sky)

•  Minimal solid colours

•  Seasonal festive backgrounds

•  Dark elegant / moody backgrounds

The chosen background is applied instantly on the home screen and remembered.

Navigation

Simple and clean bottom tab bar:

•  Home (big interactive bell)

•  Bells (grid of all available bells)

•  Backgrounds (grid of all backgrounds)

Optional small settings (volume, haptics on/off).

Design Style

•  Elegant, modern, exclusive and refined

•  Clean layout with generous spacing

•  Soft, high-quality materials and lighting

•  No clutter

•  Beautiful on both iPhone and Android

•  Support for Swedish and English language

Technical notes

•  Use device motion sensors for shake-to-ring

•  High-quality realistic bell sounds

•  Works fully offline after purchase

•  Smooth animations when switching bells or backgrounds

•  In-app purchases only for extra seasonal / premium bell packs
